Medium: a hand-painted Indian signboard illustration printed on paper: the bold simplified shapes and colours of sign painting, with the texture of old letterpress print. Flat 2D illustration, not photorealistic, not a 3D render.
Line: thick, slightly irregular dark-brown brush outlines around every object and figure; hand-made and a little imperfect.
Colour: flat fills from a matchbox-label palette: marigold yellow, vermilion red, deep indigo, leaf green, warm cream, earth brown, ink black. No gradients. Keep the colour balanced: deep indigo and leaf green must appear clearly, so the image is never dominated by orange and ochre.
Shading: shadows are flat cool indigo-tinted shapes, not brown; sparse halftone dots and a little hatching inside shadow shapes; subtle paper grain across the image; very slight colour misregistration.
Light: warm late-afternoon sunlight from the top-left, shadows falling to the bottom-right.
Camera (unless the subject says otherwise): surfaces such as a charpai or desk are seen from above; every object resting on them is drawn at the same gentle three-quarter tilt, so its top and front face are both visible; things hanging on a trunk or wall face the viewer.
Foliage: every tree keeps its own natural leaf shape. Neem leaves are long pinnate compound leaves, small serrated leaflets lined up in pairs along a central stalk, in drooping sprays; banyan and peepal leaves are broad, simple and oval or heart-shaped; mango leaves are long and simple. No leaves ever fan out from a single point. Shade from foliage falls as soft rounded dappled patches, never as sharp leaf-shaped silhouettes.
Setting: present-day rural Bihar, north India (2020s); only objects, clothes and technology of today.
Mood: warm, tactile and full of character; graphic like a sign painting, textured like old print.
