# MANDATE style bible

Locked 2026-11-11 (direction C). The exact wording sent to the model is `style-block.txt`; this file
explains it for humans. Golden references in `references/`: `scene-village-desk.png` (master desk)
and `character-pa.png` (master character). Every generation attaches them.

## Look in one line

A hand-painted Indian signboard, printed on old paper: bold simple shapes, thick brown brush
outlines, flat matchbox-label colours, cool indigo shadows, a little halftone and paper grain.

## Rules

- **Line:** thick, slightly uneven dark-brown outlines around every object and figure.
- **Fill:** flat colour, no gradients. Shading = one flat cool indigo-tinted shadow shape, with sparse halftone dots and hatching inside it.
- **Texture:** subtle paper grain everywhere; very slight colour misregistration.
- **Light:** warm late afternoon from the **top-left**, always. Shadows fall bottom-right.
- **Camera:** surfaces seen from above; objects on them at one gentle three-quarter tilt (top + front face visible); hanging things face the viewer. Scenes visited in person (chaupal, rally, counting) are eye level. The village map uses the same high three-quarter view as the desk.
- **Foliage:** every tree keeps its own leaf shape. Neem = pinnate sprays (leaflets in pairs along a stalk). Nothing ever fans out from one point. Foliage shade = soft round dappled patches, never leaf silhouettes.
- **Era:** present-day rural Bihar. Smartphones, plastic chairs, e-rickshaws, hoodies; no rotary phones.
- **People:** fictional and respectful, never caricatures. Dress, age, work and identity markers vary within every group so that appearance never predicts a voting bloc.
- **Characters:** chest-up, three-quarter or near-front view, plain cream background, 2×2 sheet in the order neutral, pleased, angry, suspicious.

## Banned

- Text, letters or numbers in any image (the game draws all text live). Papers, banners, boards and screens stay blank.
- Real politicians or recognisable real people.
- Party symbols or emblems (lotus, hand, broom, elephant, lantern, bicycle-as-emblem, etc.) and real party flag colour combinations.
- Logos, watermarks, signatures.
- Fan-shaped leaves; leaf-silhouette shadows.
